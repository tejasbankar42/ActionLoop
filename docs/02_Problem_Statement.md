# 02 · Problem Statement

Administrative teams in colleges, clinics, small businesses and offices spend a large share of their day on repetitive document handling:

- **Manual data entry** — fields from invoices, forms and receipts are retyped into spreadsheets or ERPs.
- **Late error discovery** — missing signatures, guardian details or GSTINs are discovered after an action was already taken.
- **Duplicate processing** — the same vendor invoice can be submitted twice and paid twice.
- **Slow routing** — complaints wait in inboxes until someone decides which department owns them.
- **Weak accountability** — when something goes wrong, there is no trustworthy record of who approved what and when; spreadsheet logs can be edited silently.

Generic AI tools make extraction faster but introduce a new risk: model output being trusted blindly for decisions with financial or personal impact.

**Goal:** automate the routine parts while keeping humans in control of consequential decisions, and make the decision trail independently verifiable.
